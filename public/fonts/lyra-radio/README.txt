Lyra Radio typography

Barlow Condensed 700/800 normal and Space Grotesk 400/500/600/700 normal.
Original Google Fonts WOFF2 subsets are served locally for predictable offline rendering.
All downloaded weights, source URLs, SHA256 hashes and official metadata are recorded in
docs/design/lyra-radio/fonts/sources.json. The matching OFL notices accompany these files.
assets/fonts.css defines the Latin, Latin Extended and Vietnamese subsets returned by Google.
